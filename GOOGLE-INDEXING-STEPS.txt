GOOGLE SEARCH SETUP

1. Open Google Search Console.
2. Choose “URL prefix.”
3. Enter: https://radmantt.github.io/portfolio/
4. Complete one of Google's offered ownership-verification methods.
5. Open Sitemaps and submit: sitemap.xml
6. Open URL Inspection, enter the homepage URL, and select Request Indexing.
7. Add the portfolio URL to LinkedIn, your GitHub profile, and your resume.

Google controls crawling and ranking, so no one can guarantee an exact position or indexing date. A distinctive full name, correct metadata, a sitemap, and relevant links improve discoverability.
